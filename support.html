Getting started

Milanes opens with a demo home so you can explore it. To control your own devices, open Settings → Connections and connect one or more services.

Smart Life / Tuya

Create a free Smart Home cloud project at platform.tuya.com on the data centre that matches your Smart Life account's region, link your Smart Life account by scanning the QR code under Devices → Link App Account, then enter the project's Access ID and Secret and your account UID in the app. To unlock doors remotely, also authorise Smart Lock Open Service for the project and allow remote unlock on the lock.

eWeLink (SONOFF)

Create a developer app at dev.ewelink.cc, set its Redirect URL to the one shown in the app, then sign in with your eWeLink account. For gates, turn on inching (pulse) mode on the relay.

TTLock

Create an application at euopen.ttlock.com and sign in with your TTLock app account. Remote lock and unlock need a TTLock gateway near the lock.

Safety

Gates open and doors unlock only with a press-and-hold, never a single tap. Always make sure the area around a gate is clear before opening it.
