Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026

Milanes is a home app that controls smart devices you already own. It is built to keep your information on your device.

We don't collect your data

There is no Milanes account and no Milanes server. The developer does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information, usage data, analytics or device data. The app contains no advertising and no tracking.

Services you choose to connect

If you connect Smart Life / Tuya, eWeLink or TTLock, the app sends requests directly from your device to that service's cloud to read and control your devices. Those requests are governed by the provider's own privacy policy. The credentials and sign-in tokens you enter are stored only in your device's secure keychain and are sent only to the service they belong to. For TTLock, your password is used once to sign in and is not stored.

On your device

Preferences such as your theme, favourites and which relays are gates are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app, or disconnecting a service in Settings, removes the related data from the device.

Children

Milanes is not directed to children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
